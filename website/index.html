Frequently asked questions

Is FlowAccess really free?

Yes. Every account gets a free daily session allowance tracked by a smart timer. There are no subscriptions and no hidden fees.

What is Google Flow?

Google Flow is Google's AI filmmaking tool (from Google Labs) that turns text prompts into cinematic video clips using models like Veo 3.1. FlowAccess gives you managed access to it.

Why do I need a new Chrome profile?

FlowAccess runs in an isolated Chrome profile so your personal Google account, cookies and data are never touched. It's a one-minute setup and keeps everything clean and safe.

How much time do I get per day?

Your daily limit is set by the administrator (commonly a few hours per 24-hour period). The dashboard timer shows exactly how much remains, and you can pause anytime.

Can I use two sessions at once?

No — each account is limited to one active session at a time, even across different Chrome profiles. This keeps access fair for everyone.
